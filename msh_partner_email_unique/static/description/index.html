Unique Partner Email

Prevent duplicate contact emails with a PostgreSQL unique index. Case and surrounding spaces are ignored. Blank addresses are allowed.

Features

  • Works with contact forms, CSV imports and concurrent transactions.
  • Includes archived contacts and contacts in every company.
  • No external services or configuration required.
  • Installation stops if existing email duplicates need correction.

Installation

Copy this addon into your Odoo 19 addons path, update the Apps list and install it. See README.rst for operation and validation.
